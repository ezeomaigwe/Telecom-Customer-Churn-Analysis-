# Telecom-Customer-Churn-Analysis-
Audited a 100-record telecom dataset to confirm synthetic origin (sequential IDs, uniform ranges, zero missing values) against academic literature on simulated data; documented resulting generalizability limits and connected the team's measurement integrity and stewardship practices to applied ethical principles guiding the analysis.
A graduate Business Analytics capstone analyzing a 100-customer telecom dataset to identify churn drivers, using descriptive statistics, probability/distribution modeling, and confidence intervals. The team found a 28% churn rate concentrated in customers with low service counts (1–2 services churned 100%; 4+ services churned 0%) and early tenure (months 6–10), and delivered retention recommendations around early intervention, service bundling, and age-specific campaigns.
